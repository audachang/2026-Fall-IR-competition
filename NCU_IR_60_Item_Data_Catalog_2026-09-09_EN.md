# NCU IR Catalog of 60 Data Items

Inspection date: September 9, 2026, Asia/Taipei. Source: the authenticated [Integrated Query Platform](https://cis.ncu.edu.tw/IRSys/dataMarket/dataPlatform), inspected read-only using the Codex built-in browser.

**The field pages for all 60 items below were opened; their names, request fields, and time options were readable, and all displayed an “Add dataset of interest” (加入有興趣資料集) button. No application was submitted, no data values were read, and no downloadable dataset was verified.** This catalog is neither permission to redistribute the material as public data nor a complete data dictionary. Fields are selected highlights, not full transcriptions.

Time ranges indicate only the outer bounds of selectable options. **They do not mean that every intervening period has an option or data.** Parenthesized counts exclude the “Please select” placeholder but include any separately noted blank or special values. All data levels are inferred from fields; administrators must confirm row definitions and unique keys.

*Translated September 13, 2026 from [the Chinese catalog](NCU_IR_60項資料目錄_2026-09-09.md). The inspection statements describe September 9, not a new platform check. English dataset titles are descriptive translations. ROC years, semester/reporting codes, dataset identifiers, irregular values, and source URLs are preserved. “Program type” refers to 學制; “occurrences” may count a person more than once, unlike a distinct headcount.*
