## English

An independent study course at National Central University in Academic Year 115, Semester 1. Students develop an institutional research project using NCU IR data, with NCU’s “2026 AI Applications for University Governance Competition” (2026 AI校務治理實務競賽) as an intermediate venue for presenting outcomes. Classes meet on Thursdays, 13:00–16:00, in S5-601.

### Versions and Data Use

The English Markdown documents were translated from the Chinese documents on September 13, 2026. They retain the source versions’ dates, requirements, and evidence limits; they do not represent a new check of websites, platform access, competition results, or rules. English competition categories, project titles, and document titles are descriptive translations rather than verified official names. Chinese interface labels and dataset codes are retained where needed to locate records.

The catalog and field descriptions are extracts of information visible after login. They are for course participants within the approved scope and do not authorize redistribution as public data. Actual analytical data must be requested through the IR Office. Translation does not expand the permitted scope of use or public release.
