# Past Institutional Research Competition Awards and Corresponding NCU IR Data

Verification date: September 9, 2026. Scope: public competition announcements from National Central University, National United University, Chung Shan Medical University, and Mackay Medical College / Mackay Medical University. This report includes **55 award records**, including ties, award tiers, and separate selection stages. These are not 55 distinct research projects, nor a complete register covering every year at every university in Taiwan.

**Conclusion: NCU IR has a stronger data foundation for questions involving courses, grades, enrollment records, and UCAN. Individual employment follow-up, needs and satisfaction surveys, administrative operations, transportation, energy, and finance generally still lack core data in the reviewed catalog. Data used by past NCU award-winning projects are not necessarily listed in the current data marketplace.**

*Translated September 13, 2026 from [the Chinese comparison](校務研究競賽歷年得獎題目與NCU資料對照_2026-09-09.md). Verification statements and coverage describe the original September 9 review; no fresh award search is implied. English project titles, source titles, and award-tier names are descriptive translations, not verified official English wording. Original record IDs, source URLs, ties, stages, and support ratings are retained. Chinese wording is retained where needed to identify an ambiguous title or award tier.*

## Verification Conventions

- Award ranks and original titles follow official university announcements. For National United University in 2023, awardees/units were matched to the final-round agenda to identify titles. Images in the 2024 National United University and 2026 Mackay announcements were also read.
- NCU’s old site had been redesigned; tested old Joomla announcement URLs returned 404. Some historical text remained available in search-engine indexes of official pages. Original URLs and cross-checking sources are retained below; indexed text is not described as a currently accessible webpage. Ranks were not inferred from award images that could not be read.
- “Required data” represents research design judgments based on titles and public summaries. **Unless explicitly attributed to an announcement or competition rules, it does not imply that full award-winning papers were read or that the authors actually used every listed variable.**
- The NCU comparison uses the 60 field catalogs inspected individually in the original work: 38 administrative-system datasets and 22 ministry reports. See the [60-item catalog](NCU_IR_60_Item_Data_Catalog_2026-09-09_EN.md). Only field and application-period information was obtained, not values, record counts, or approval.
- The comparison assesses the data conditions for adapting an idea to NCU. NCU’s catalog does not contain individual student records from other institutions, and national comparisons cannot rely solely on NCU data.
- Across all tables, anonymous individual linkage keys, actual overlapping years, missingness, code definitions, and permitted scope remain unconfirmed. Listed fields are not equivalent to an analysis-ready file.

| Code | Data support judgment |
| --- | --- |
| A | Most core research fields are present; anonymous linkage, years, classifications, and actual quality still require confirmation. |
| B | Partially feasible; important outcomes, explanatory variables, or parts of the study population are missing. Add data or narrow the question. |
| C | Core data were not identified in the current 60-item catalog; this does not establish their absence from all university systems. |
| D | The announced title provides insufficient information to determine the actual question and data requirements reliably. |

### Three Data Gaps Most Consequential for Topic Selection

1. **Individual graduate follow-up.** The 2020 and 2022 NCU competitions provided graduate questionnaires, but these were not among the current 60 items. Ask whether another application route can provide salary, unemployment duration, job match, further-study outcomes, and anonymous course linkage. Do not assign each student their department’s mean salary and regress it as an individual salary.
2. **Intervention and measurement.** Teaching reform, USR, advising, and PLC questions require who participated, when, how intensively, and suitable outcome measures. Grades or SDG flags alone usually cannot identify the intervention or establish causal effects.
3. **Administrative and spatial operations.** Core tables for library borrowing, document-handling hours, financial statements, patent maintenance, dormitories, transport, energy, and tree inventories were not identified. Seek responsible operational units or external public sources.

## 6. Direct Recommendations for Current Topic Selection

| Priority | Question that could be developed at NCU | Initial data sources | One key item to obtain first |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High | Relationships between general education domains / cross-college course combinations and subsequent academic performance. | Offerings, individual course grades, semester averages, student affiliations. | Stable anonymous linkage and domain/interdisciplinary classifications. |
| High | Relationships between Holland career interests and grades in different course types. | UCAN Holland, offerings, course grades. | Matchable assessment dates and course semesters for the same student. |
| High | Prior participation and later performance in sequential courses. | Individual course grades, offerings, student background. | Course-sequence/prerequisite mapping; initially limit interpretation to association. |
| Medium | Changes in academic warning/dismissal policies and course-taking behavior. | Warnings, course grades, semester averages, leave/dismissal records. | Historical effective dates, eligible populations, and a comparison design. |
| Additional data needed | Whether interdisciplinary course-taking relates to individual salary or job fit. | Courses and grades. | Individual graduate follow-up; aggregate employment tables are insufficient. |
| Additional data needed | Relationships between travel distances between classes and semester averages. | Courses and semester averages. | Weekday/period/classroom for every course, room coordinates, and route distances. |

The originally proposed question about “total distance between classrooms and semester average grades” has related precedents in transport/accessibility award topics, but an identical winning topic was not found. If the intended construct is travel burden, calculate distances between consecutive classes in each day’s actual weekly sequence. Summing distances between every pair of classrooms measures a different form of spatial dispersion. Without timetable order and room information, the existing grade tables are insufficient.
